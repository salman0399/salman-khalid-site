---
title: Why I am starting this blog
date: 2026-10-01
summary: A home for short notes on economics, policy and method that do not fit in a journal article or an op-ed.
tags: [notes]
---

Most of what economists learn never makes it into print. A referee report gets written, a coefficient refuses to behave, a budget speech changes one line that matters for millions of salaried workers, and the thought ends up in a notebook. This blog is where those notes will go.

Expect three kinds of posts. The first is policy, mostly Pakistan's fiscal choices and what the numbers imply for households. The second is method, practical notes on difference in differences, staggered adoption and the estimators that have reshaped applied work over the past few years. The third is teaching, with lab walkthroughs and datasets my students and I use in class.

Posts will be short and, where possible, come with the code. If something here is useful or wrong, I would like to hear about it.
